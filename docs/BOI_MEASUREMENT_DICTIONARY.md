## Reading the definitions

Each entry records its question, formula, units, signal/baseline, support/exposure, missingness/identity policy, claim limits, implementing functions and a teaching example. Examples are arithmetic illustrations, not cohort observations. Full per-entry definitions are in the JSON and in the MATLAB-generated per-run guide.

## Standing implementation gaps

- Static tissue and uniform frame intervals are current assumptions. Acquisition exposure duration is not automatically equal to the frame interval. Dynamic valid tissue-time, motion exclusions and timestamp support require R1 decisions.
- Native mask occupancy now exports its covered area-time numerator, analyzed tissue-time denominator and per-frame contributions in normal statistics outputs. The input contract makes static tissue and uniform timing assumptions explicit; it does not establish scientific validity.
- An event beginning at acquisition frame 1 is currently counted as an onset. This is descriptive detector behavior; a true new-onset claim needs an explicit censoring/admission policy.
- Event projected area can include pixels outside estimated tissue. Occupancy intersects native masks with tissue. Fixed union amplitude footprints, native mean areas and refined sink durations are distinct supports.
- Physiological recovery, primary outcome admission, acquisition strata, held-out roles, comparison-specific statistics and numerical scientific acceptance criteria remain open.
- Empirical filters and geometric/temporal defaults remain revisable assumptions. Validation must cover weak, strong, brief, sustained, irregular, recurrent, overlapping and spatially changing signals, and report both gains and losses.

## Definition changes

Bump the dictionary version when a formula, support, status or interpretation changes. Calculation changes also require the appropriate pipeline contract version. Do not change scientific definitions merely to match a historical count or expected effect. Version 0.2 adds input/QC and denominator traceability; the first 0.1 export remains preserved in the walkthrough evidence. Existing valid-input detector and measurement formulas are unchanged. Inconsistent inputs cannot become valid zeros.

The 0.3.0 draft documents separately saved sink and surge window measures and
frame ingredients. Each sign retains its own tissue support and missingness.
No surge amplitude-area-time composite is defined. Proposed outcome priorities,
scientific admission and animal aggregation policy are unchanged. Historical
exports retain their original dictionary; this update does not bind new
definitions retrospectively to those results.

## Run-specific detection support

[Restricted ROI development support](BOI_STRICT_ROI_WORKFLOW.md),
R1-C02-STRICT-032, changes which pixels enter detection and its resulting event
population. Dictionary 0.3.0-draft measurement formulas remain unchanged. Every
export must be read with its saved detection profile and support contract;
whole-image and restricted recordings are not pooled. Synthetic implementation
checks do not establish physiological relevance or primary-outcome acceptance.
