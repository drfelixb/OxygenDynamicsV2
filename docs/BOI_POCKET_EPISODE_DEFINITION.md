# Working definition of a pocket episode

10 November 2026. Decision POCKET-EPISODE-DEFINITION-01; owner root, no
delegation. Researcher agreement, design only. The preceding experimental
detector remains closed and unadopted.

## Agreed principle

**Several minima within one spatially coherent, unrecovered depression should
belong to one pocket episode.**

The researcher agrees this likely represents a single pocket episode. This
is a working biological interpretation. It does not establish exact spatial,
onset, recovery or reference criteria, or authorize another detector run.
The [decision record](../../reference-validation/boi-episode-definition-review-20261110/decision.json)
preserves the agreement separately from automatic and reviewed results.

## Consequences for the next design

- A new minimum alone does not create a new episode. Preserve individual minima
  as descriptive features within the episode.
- Credible intervening recovery or a distinct spatial origin can support a
  separate event. Uncertain continuity/recovery remains explicitly uncertain.
- Spatial proximity and similar timing alone do not establish one pocket.
  A shared regional decline may contain several distinct local episodes.
- Capillary gaps should not automatically break coherent tissue activity into
  separate events. Conversely, a connecting chain of small proposals must not
  merge unrelated changes through a common regional response.
- Retain the existing proposal groups and their scale/route alternatives as
  evidence for one episode record. Recover the full decline and return using
  adequate context; the strongest representative's narrow fitting interval is
  insufficient to determine the whole episode.
- Use consistent comparison pixels to judge signal recovery, while describing
  changing spatial extent separately. Coarse proposal footprints remain
  diagnostic support, not accepted native pocket area.
- Each distinct event still needs its own scientific reference assessment.
  Recognition of an episode does not by itself resolve its measurement baseline,
  amplitude, complete duration or mean area.

## Concrete limitation established during the saved-card review

The frozen implementation retains scale alternatives, then fits one
representative seed. Its local phase search is limited to the seed trough
plus/minus the selected span. These are the selected examples, not new fits:

| Saved card | Selected phase-search window | Proposed interval | Reviewed/discussed context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FB2413 site 3/event 6 | 831–847 | 832–840 | Approximately 826–851; measurement baseline unresolved |
| FB2412 site 20/event 1 | 110–174 | 117–169 | Reviewed 114–182, baseline 104–113 |
| FB2407 site 12/event 7 | 598–662 | 611–652 | Earlier decline and prominent rise around 660 |

The selected FB2413 fit cannot reach the discussed onset/recovery, and the
selected FB2412 fit cannot reach reviewed recovery 182. This is a structural
window restriction in addition to background/reference and endpoint-threshold
limitations. It does not establish the correct timing on a different footprint.
An assembly design should retain longer-context evidence without blindly taking
the earliest start and latest end of all overlapping proposals.

## Open requirements and next step

Specify how spatial continuity, recovered intervals, region-wide activity,
vascular/boundary variation and competing episode identities will be represented.
Keep observed, relative and unresolved recovery distinct. Assess the approach
against the broad existing benchmark rather than using only two positive cases.
Prepare a finite implementation/acceptance plan before changing the method.
No numerical thresholds, new test campaign or subsequent implementation is
approved by this definition agreement.

The burden formula, normalization, strict complete-contribution rule, original
recordings/results, reviewed references/decisions and existing exports remain
unchanged. No partial burden, imputed zero area or scientific recalculation is
introduced. The five reserved recordings remain outside new processing.
