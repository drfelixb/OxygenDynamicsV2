## Candidate model and choices

Begin with multiscale temporal proposals followed by joint segmented fits of the surrounding background and episode. Compare a depression-and-recovery explanation with smooth trend and persistent-transition alternatives. Use several episode shapes with flexible phase lengths, not a single averaged template. Recurrent minima may belong to one episode or separate recovered episodes; preserve competing interpretations when evidence is insufficient.

Record a finite numerical search design before evaluating recordings: one primary configuration and no more than three prespecified robustness alternatives. State duration/search limits, smoothing/complexity constraints, score penalties, missing-sample handling and numerical conditioning explicitly. They are computational/model assumptions, not universal biological thresholds. Do not change these choices to reproduce a particular accepted boundary or to complete burden values. An insufficient duration range produces a documented limitation rather than a silent rejection.

The first prototype keeps the saved correction fixed. Raw/corrected disagreement remains an explicit output, not a requirement for both stages to agree. Shared or broad depressions remain eligible; neither spatial breadth nor a slow shape alone proves background. If a flexible background and an episode explain the signal comparably, retain uncertainty. Saved native support is supporting evidence produced by the old detector, not ground truth.

Outputs include candidate identity, source hashes, footprint origin, sampling rate, proposed timing and plausible alternatives, observed baseline/decline/recovery evidence, competing fit scores and reasons for candidate/background-like/uncertain or incomplete classification. Keep scores and display-only fitted background separate from approved measurement references. Unknown support stays unavailable; missing samples cannot be bridged to assert recovery.

## Finite evaluation

Reuse the locked 12-development/five-held-out partition. Develop and compare configurations only on the 12 development recordings. Freeze source, settings and selection rules before the single held-out evaluation. The five recordings are a model-development holdout, not historically unseen data; their earlier unlabelled shape projections and overviews have been inspected. Do not claim an untouched independent validation set or tune from the held-out results.

Use at most 24 named synthetic fixtures covering isolated/short/long/asymmetric episodes, overshoot without a plateau, recurrence with and without recovery, drift, flat/noisy/monotonic/step backgrounds, raw/corrected disagreement, recording boundaries, missing data and sampling-rate/input identity guards. These are comparison outputs, not sensitivity/specificity against the automatic labels.
