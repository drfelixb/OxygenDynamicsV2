**Result: incomplete before acknowledgement.** The one launch ran **17.1546 seconds** and stopped at `process_policy_guard_error_RuntimeError: Process 23091 became unidentifiable during observation`. A saved `/bin/ps` row shows PID 23091 as `(java_home)`, parent 22859, UID 501 and PGID 22857 in the new MATLAB group. The guard could not obtain its precise libproc birth identity, so it did not add that PID to the verified registry and stopped before a ready receipt or token. Disappearance between `/bin/ps` and libproc is plausible for a short-lived child, but the saved evidence cannot establish whether it exited at that instant or why identity lookup failed.

The [summary](../../reference-validation/software-g5-r07-startup-only-20260928/summary.json), [trigger process tables](../../reference-validation/software-g5-r07-startup-only-20260928/evidence/trigger.json) and [stop report](../../reference-validation/software-g5-r07-startup-only-20260928/evidence/stop-report.json) are preserved. The worker's startup log is empty. Therefore the live acknowledgement was **not demonstrated**; no `ui.Run`, detector, statistics or recorded-movie analysis was invoked by this startup-only path.

**Cleanup and uncertainty.** The guard refused a group-wide signal because PID 23091 was an unverified group member. It sent SIGTERM to two precisely verified PIDs: group leader 22857 and MATLAB process 22859. The stop report recorded **zero remaining verified live PIDs** and two clean snapshots, but its formal outcome is `containment_failure` because it retained `Original group has an unverified member`. A separate read-only process snapshot after the supervisor exited found no live original group, no saved tracked identity and no PID 23091. This later observation supports that no process remained then; it does not turn the stop-time uncertainty into verified containment. No unrelated process was signalled.

Peak packet bytes were **0.429 MiB** against the 28 MiB stop; minimum sampled free space was **122.51 GiB** against the 8 GiB floor. No sampled resource threshold was exceeded. The proposed recording output remained absent.

This attempt establishes a live **fail-closed pre-acknowledgement stop**, not a successful handshake or verified containment. The one-launch budget is exhausted. Another startup or any recorded-movie attempt needs a new bounded researcher decision after review of this saved identity race. SW-G5-07, G5 acceptance and release remain open and unapproved.
