## Requirements to carry into the next detector discussion

- Use the varied video contexts before choosing another timing/noise rule.
  The views visibly differ in raw scale, capillary pattern and recording-wide
  trend; matching two familiar trajectories is inadequate evidence.
- Assess spatial extent, local change and surrounding response through time.
  Compare regional activity with regional activity plus a local episode, rather
  than treating every shared response as removable noise.
- Keep fast variability separate from structured biological changes, and keep
  raw/corrected disagreement explicit. A corrected-channel failure should not
  automatically veto a visible raw-video candidate.
- Preserve recurrent, flat-trough, asymmetric and edge cases with an uncertain
  outcome. Recognition and approximate timing are separate from an accepted
  scientific amplitude reference, area and complete burden.
- Obtain whole-episode researcher judgments on the starting board. Unreviewed
  and uncertain material cannot supply invented positives/negatives. Freeze a
  later detector before using the five reserved recordings; do not tune to them.
