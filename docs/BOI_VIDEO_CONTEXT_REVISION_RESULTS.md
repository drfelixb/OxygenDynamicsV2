# Recovering references and regional responses: prototype results

7 November 2026. The approved two-window revision is implemented and delivered as an isolated Python experiment. FB2412 now supports an additional local dip within a responding region. FB2413 retains evidence for the later raw decline, while regional context and its measurement reference remain unresolved. [Open the two comparison cards](../../reference-validation/boi-video-context-revision-20261107/review.html).

## What changed

The prototype compares a preceding recovery and subsequent decline on each archived fixed cohort. An increasing-then-decreasing trajectory fit supplies an onset and comparable-fit alternatives. Robust constant-versus-linear comparisons use samples strictly before the chosen onset to describe stable, recovering or changing preceding signal. Recognition context and measurement-reference eligibility are separate fields. No fitted trajectory becomes a scientific amplitude reference.

Surrounding patches now have explicit episode-specific roles: quiet supported tissue, responding regional tissue, peripheral acquisition checks, limited saved support or uncertain response. Membership is fixed during each comparison. Responding patches estimate a regional waveform on one subset and check its agreement on another. Additional local decline and rise require raw evidence, residual evidence and spatial corroboration. Each eligible patch is omitted in turn to expose influential comparator choices.

The archived patch geometry and signal-confidence eligibility remain in use. Saved-mask coverage qualifies comparator support; that mask's anatomical accuracy is unvalidated. Peripheral and limited-support patches remain visible as checks and do not fit the regional component. No recording ID, reviewed baseline, timing cue, saved footprint or patch-82 label enters the assessment rule. Those annotations are compared after inference; displayed matches follow the frozen time-alignment and footprint-overlap rule.

## Important limitations

Onset alternatives describe comparable trajectory-fit costs; they are not calibrated confidence intervals. A flat baseline permits many equal-cost splits. In the noiseless stable constructed case, the earliest selected onset is frame 10 although the imposed decline begins much later; the alternatives extend through frame 76. The eight checks verify stated recognition and uncertainty behaviours, not accurate onset estimation. The earlier FB2413 boundary is another clear unresolved boundary problem. No event duration should be accepted from these point estimates.

The return test uses a five-sample median with tolerance equal to the larger of four empirical variation units and one quarter of the fall. For FB2413 this allows 148.8 units against a 152.6-unit fall; for FB2412, 27.6 against 63.8. The reported first return windows satisfy that computational rule. They do not establish complete biological recovery, a stable recovered baseline or a revised measurement endpoint. Raw rise, absolute return and relative return remain separate evidence fields. The cards make the tolerance and experimental status explicit.

The regional model assumes that the median fractional trajectory of selected responding patches can describe a regional contribution at the candidate. A held-out patch subset tests waveform agreement within this comparison; it does not prove an acquisition cause, biological identity or unique decomposition. FB2413 fails even this agreement test. The displayed residual there remains diagnostic.

The runs reuse 30,108 FB2413 and 42,624 FB2412 archived hypotheses, including overlapping alternatives, in the same windows 608–1114 and 5–839. They generate no new proposals. Counts are not pocket counts, sensitivity or specificity. These familiar regression examples provide no independent biological validation. Detection, boundary accuracy, measurement reference eligibility and event area remain distinct questions. The final production language remains undecided.

## Verification and preservation

All eight constructed checks pass: stable local cycle, renewed local decline during recovery, recovery without an added event, linked dips, uneven regional change, a local event within regional change, a misleading limited-support comparator and unavailable phase evidence. A fixed-onset preceding-fit check is invariant to changes at and after that onset.

One engineering repair was used before freezing. Initial checks passed 5/8 because an arbitrary preceding low could borrow an earlier decline. Anchoring to the last observed preceding valley and requiring an actual fall toward the candidate trough produced 7/8. The remaining assertion incorrectly required independent-event status during incomplete recovery. It was corrected to require retained local evidence with linked-episode uncertainty, while strengthening raw and residual evidence assertions. The corrected eight cases passed before either recording was run. Initial and intermediate failures are retained; no real-example retuning followed.

Independent selected verification reproduces four geometries, 66 fixed raw/comparator traces, eight preceding fits, 245 alternative onset costs using SciPy isotonic regression, four raw return searches and 78 patch-role decisions. A separate implementation reproduces the four regional/local component arrays, held-out correlations, component metrics and all 17 comparator-omission decisions. All 72,732 hypothesis identities, masks and previous states match their source catalogues. Both figures were visually inspected. Gallery assets and final source/result preservation are recorded in the [verification receipt](../../reference-validation/boi-video-context-revision-20261107/verification.json).

The delivery uses three batches, one frozen configuration and one repair. Successful workers peak at about 378 MiB, below 2 GiB; derived evidence is about 90 MiB, below 256 MiB. The final preservation check covers 1,328 protected files, including four original movie files and the accepted scientific artifacts. Prior planning snapshots remain pinned. The burden formula, normalization, complete-burden missingness rule, original recordings, automatic/reviewed measurements, export formats and production MATLAB workflow are unchanged. No new scientific result, burden value, partial sum, GUI integration, Git transport or publication was produced.

The experimental milestone is delivered and stopped. Review the two cards as a whole. Any subsequent work should address boundary identifiability and meaningful recovery before accepting event durations or adopting this detector; further implementation needs its own agreed scope.
