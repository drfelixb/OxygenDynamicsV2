## Demonstrated behaviour

The isolated `prototypes/boi_episode_components` module records three separate
facts for each source: local episode evidence, regional change evidence and a
downstream measurement-reference audit. All twelve development recordings,
128,931 original candidate records and 320,824 original seeds are accounted for.
Every source has an explicit spatial assessment attempt, including sources
with unresolved onset/reference or strong surrounding responses. An observed
spatial deficit alone does not establish biological episode identity.

Local and regional responses can coexist. Reference-audit availability cannot
change detector boundaries, identity, spatial evidence or grouping. The actual
20-only, 10-only and withheld audit paths give identical detection/grouping
outputs on the fixed constructed contract case. All experimental records remain
measurement-ready and burden-ready false.

Detection still needs its own diagnostic before/low/after context. Separating
the scientific reference audit does not establish that detector context as
physiologically recovered tissue or solve its selection. Local turning points,
parent depressions and conditional surrounding comparisons can still choose
an inappropriate episode. The saved real examples show this clearly.
