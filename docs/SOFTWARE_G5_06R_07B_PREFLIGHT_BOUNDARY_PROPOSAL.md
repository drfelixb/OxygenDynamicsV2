## Revised boundary

The former caller captured `final_snap`, performed another potentially slow `preflight()`, and then passed the already captured table into `GuardedRunController.consider_release()`, which called `preflight_ok()` again. The new live caller uses `consider_release_fresh(snapshot_provider)`. The controller completes its own preflight **before** invoking that provider, records the completion time, and rejects a final snapshot timestamp earlier than completion. The provider then captures a new table, checks its gap from the preceding sample and its sampled RSS without another source/free-space/packet preflight, and returns it for immediate guard scan and policy assessment. The controller checks age before claim reservation and again before the token sink. The startup-only writer checks age immediately before token-file publication. A failed age check after claim reservation leaves the claim consumed and publishes no token. The old object-taking `consider_release` remains for historical saved-fixture callers; the live SW-G5-06R-07 path uses only the fresh API.
