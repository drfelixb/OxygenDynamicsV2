## What changed and what the checks mean

The isolated `prototypes/boi_episode_identity` module retains every original
candidate/seed identity, mask, scale and route. The original catalogue anchor
is retained rather than recentered on another minimum. It evaluates at most
two identity/reference refinement rounds, with local 20/10-sample diagnostic
windows, raw and fixed surrounding comparisons, and observed coarse spatial
continuation/branch returns. Alternatives and conflicting certainty are
serialized directly in the source output, without a reporting repair overlay.

The new anchor constraint prevents a claimed connected interval ending before
its source anchor. **Containment of an anchor is only a necessary check.** It
does not establish that a long interval represents the intended biological
episode. Context extension can still join a long background decline to a later
pocket. Reference windows adjacent to a wrongly early onset can consequently
remain remote from the intended later event.

Twenty- and ten-sample windows are assessed individually on the same pixels.
Short-only references stay conditional, and unsupported absolute returns stay
unavailable. A provisional endpoint is saved separately from a claimed raw
absolute return. These changes preserve missingness; they do not establish
the biological suitability of a window or calculate a new amplitude/burden.

Actual coarse-block deficit/return evidence and temporal spatial links are
recorded with branch cohorts, extent, field-response and confounding indicators.
Native snapshots/movies remain the earlier prepared evidence. The new eighteen
coarse phase-map panels are diagnostic observations, not native segmentation,
anatomical validation or accepted pocket area.

## Keep, change and stop

Keep the source/seed provenance, explicit confidence contract, withheld
short-reference certainty, identical-cohort comparisons and coarse spatial
phase evidence. They make the remaining failures traceable.

Do not adopt this configuration as the production detector or as input to
scientific burden. The present context-extension/level-band construction can
still combine earlier activity and a later pocket; a reference local to that
wrong onset does not solve the problem. The conditional regional route can
also fail to retain a recognized local component. Coarse confounding indicators
do not establish native tissue identity.

The bounded implementation, evaluation and reporting are complete and stopped
at this documented quality checkpoint. A further method revision requires
review of these failures and a separate finite decision. No further campaign,
scientific method adoption, MATLAB integration, Git transport or publication
is active.
